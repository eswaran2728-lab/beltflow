Install BeltFlow on Your Device

BeltFlow is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to provide a full native app experience with zero app store delays, automatic updates, and offline support.

Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet)
  1. Tap the "Install App" button at the top of the screen.
  2. If no prompt appears, tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu in the browser top-right.
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (box with an upward arrow ⎋) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add" in the top right to install the standalone icon.
Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

Click the install icon ⊕ in your browser's address bar to launch BeltFlow in its own dedicated, clean window.

Google Play Console Publishing Guide

🎯 Direct Path from PWA to Google Play
Because this app is now a compliant PWA with full manifest and service worker, it can be wrapped into a Google Play Trusted Web Activity (TWA) `.aab` package in minutes!
Step 1: Deploy to Vercel
Push to GitHub and connect to Vercel (or run npx vercel) to get your live HTTPS URL (e.g. https://beltflow.vercel.app).
Step 2: Generate Android Bundle with PWABuilder
Go to PWABuilder.com, enter your Vercel URL, click Build My PWA ➔ Android Package (.aab).
Step 3: Google Play Console Upload
Upload the generated .aab package directly to your Google Play Console under App Releases.
