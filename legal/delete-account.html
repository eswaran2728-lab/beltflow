How to ask for deletion (English)

You can ask for your account and personal details to be deleted at any time. You do not need to install the app to do this.

Option 1: from the app or the website

  1. Sign in to the BeltFlow app, or to this website.
  2. Open Profile (in the app) or your account menu (on the website), then Privacy and my data.
  3. Under Make a request, choose Delete my account and personal details. A parent or guardian can include their child.
  4. Send the request.

Option 2: without signing in

Send an e-mail to from the address you registered with. Write "Delete my BeltFlow account" and the name of your academy. We may ask you to confirm the request.

What happens next

  • Your academy's administrator carries out the request within 21 days.
  • Deleted: your name, e-mail address, phone number and password; your device notification settings and in-app notifications; and, for a child included in the request, the child's name, identity card number, contact details, medical and emergency information, and training videos.
  • Kept, without your name: payment and receipt records, because the law requires accounting records to be kept (generally seven years); attendance and grading records your academy needs, linked only to the anonymous record. Messages you wrote to an instructor stay in that conversation, shown as from a deleted user.
  • After deletion you can no longer sign in.

See the Privacy Notice for more about how data is handled.

Cara meminta pemadaman (Bahasa Malaysia)

Anda boleh meminta akaun dan butiran peribadi anda dipadamkan pada bila-bila masa. Anda tidak perlu memasang aplikasi untuk berbuat demikian.

Pilihan 1: melalui aplikasi atau laman web

  1. Log masuk ke aplikasi BeltFlow, atau ke laman web ini.
  2. Buka Profil (dalam aplikasi) atau menu akaun anda (di laman web), kemudian Privasi dan data saya.
  3. Di bawah Buat permintaan, pilih Padam akaun dan butiran peribadi saya. Ibu bapa atau penjaga boleh menyertakan anak mereka.
  4. Hantar permintaan.

Pilihan 2: tanpa log masuk

Hantar e-mel ke daripada alamat yang anda gunakan semasa mendaftar. Tulis "Padam akaun BeltFlow saya" dan nama akademi anda. Kami mungkin meminta anda mengesahkan permintaan tersebut.

Apa yang berlaku seterusnya

  • Pentadbir akademi anda melaksanakan permintaan dalam tempoh 21 hari.
  • Dipadamkan: nama, alamat e-mel, nombor telefon dan kata laluan anda; tetapan pemberitahuan peranti dan pemberitahuan dalam aplikasi anda; dan, bagi anak yang disertakan dalam permintaan, nama anak, nombor kad pengenalan, butiran perhubungan, maklumat kesihatan dan kecemasan, serta video latihan.
  • Disimpan, tanpa nama anda: rekod bayaran dan resit, kerana undang-undang mewajibkan rekod perakaunan disimpan (lazimnya tujuh tahun); rekod kehadiran dan keputusan ujian yang diperlukan oleh akademi, dipautkan hanya kepada rekod tanpa nama. Mesej yang anda tulis kepada jurulatih kekal dalam perbualan itu, ditunjukkan sebagai daripada pengguna yang telah dipadam.
  • Selepas pemadaman, anda tidak lagi boleh log masuk.
